# Source Models and Papers

- **Ya-TASERPS**
  - Paper: JoAnn Lee and Andrew Crooks (2021), [Youth and Their Artificial Social Environmental Risk and Promotive Scores (Ya-TASERPS): An Agent-Based Model of Interactional Theory of Delinquency](https://doi.org/10.18564/jasss.4660), *Journal of Artificial Societies and Social Simulation* 24(4), Article 2.
  - Model source: [CoMSES v1.0.0 release](https://www.comses.net/codebases/84a733a5-a885-4caa-a426-fc72dc2a6e8a/releases/1.0.0/).
  - File used in this study: [NetLogo model](yatasterps/project.nlogo), adapted from the official v1.0.0 release.
  - License: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](yatasterps/LICENSE).

- **Sakoda's checkerboard model**
  - Paper: James M. Sakoda (1971), [The checkerboard model of social interaction](https://doi.org/10.1080/0022250X.1971.9989791), *The Journal of Mathematical Sociology* 1(1), 119-132.
  - Model source: This study's implementation was reconstructed from the paper.

- **Slumulation**
  - Paper: Amit Patel, Andrew Crooks, and Naoru Koizumi (2012), [Slumulation: An Agent-Based Modeling Approach to Slum Formations](https://doi.org/10.18564/jasss.2045), *Journal of Artificial Societies and Social Simulation* 15(4), Article 2.
  - Model source: [Slumulation GitHub repository](https://github.com/acrooks2/Slumulation).
  - Files used in this study: [Slumulation_OriginalModel_NetLogo4_2.nlogo](slumulation/Slumulation_OriginalModel_NetLogo4_2.nlogo) and [Slumulation_NetLogo6_1.nlogo](slumulation/Slumulation_NetLogo6_1.nlogo).
